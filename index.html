Why We Exist

Talent is abundant, but opportunity is not always accessible.

At the Ngozi Arthur Richard (NARF), we are committed to breaking the cycle of poverty by empowering young people aged 15–35 in underserved and low-income communities through education, empowerment and opportunity.

We work to remove barriers, unlock potential and equip young people with the skills, knowledge, support and experience they need to build brighter, and more sustainable futures.

Meet our founder

Chisom Obiwuru-Okpamen

Chisom is a Human Resources professional, STEAM advocate and passionate champion of youth development and women's empowerment. Her passion for social impact began through working with children and young people from low-income communities following her pageantry success in Ghana, 2015.

Since 2020, she has independently sponsored and supported initiatives that inspire and transform young lives. This commitment led her to establish NARF, creating a platform to expand opportunities through education, skills development, mentorship and empowerment.

Her vision is to unlock potential, promote equal opportunities and build sustainable futures for young people, with an ambition to positively impact over 10,000 young people by 2037.

She believes every young person deserves the opportunity to thrive, regardless of their background or circumstances.

About Us

Building opportunity, confidence and sustainable futures for young people.

MISSION

Our mission is to break the cycle of poverty by empowering young people aged 15–35 in underserved communities through education, skills development, employability initiatives, entrepreneurship and mentorship

VISION

We envision a world where every young person has equal access to the opportunities, resources and support needed to reach their full potential.

We aspire to build inclusive and sustainable communities where young people are educated, empowered, economically independent and equipped to become tomorrow's leaders, innovators and change-makers.

OUR STORY

The Ngozi Arthur Richard Foundation (NARF) was established out of a belief that talent and potential exist in every community, but opportunity is not always equally accessible.

Across many underserved communities, young people face barriers that limit their access to quality education, employment, entrepreneurship and personal development. These challenges can prevent talented individuals from achieving their aspirations and contributing fully to society.

We believe that a young person's circumstances should never determine the limits of their future.

NARF exists to help bridge this opportunity gap: through education, employability programmes, entrepreneurship development, scholarships, mentorship and community outreach, we aim to equip young people with the knowledge, confidence, skills and networks they need to build better futures.

OUR LEADERSHIP PRIORITIES

  • Integrity and accountability: Acting responsibly and ensuring that our work reflects our values.
  • Inclusive leadership: Promoting diversity, equal participation and representation.
  • Community impact: Prioritising initiatives that respond to real challenges and create meaningful change.
  • Collaboration: Working with organisations, institutions, businesses and individuals to expand opportunities.
  • Sustainability: Building programmes and partnerships that can deliver lasting benefits.
